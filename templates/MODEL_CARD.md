# Model Card: <name>

## Version

Model version, training run reference, and the date it was promoted.

## Owner

Accountable person or team, and who approves a promotion.

## Intended Use

The decisions this model is meant to support and the population it serves.

## Out-Of-Scope Use

Uses that are known to be unsafe, unvalidated, or explicitly disallowed. This
section is the useful half of the card; a card without it is marketing.

## Training Data / Provenance / Legal Basis

Sources, collection period, consent or contractual basis for training use,
retention limits, and known coverage gaps.

## Features

Feature set, where each is computed, and whether the serving path matches the
training path.

## Evaluation Results

Metrics, the dataset and population they were measured on, and comparison
against the incumbent version.

## Fairness Assessment

Groups measured, metric used, observed gap, and the threshold agreed to block
release.

## Limitations & Known Failure Modes

Where the model degrades, what it systematically gets wrong, and inputs it
should not be trusted on.

## Explainability

What a person affected by a decision can be told, and how.

## Human Oversight

Which decisions require a person in the loop, who that person is, and what they
can override.

## Monitoring & Drift Thresholds

Signals monitored in production, threshold per signal, owner, and the action
each threshold triggers.

## Rollback Criteria

What reverts this version, who decides, and how fast reversion can happen.

## Approval Record

Who approved, on what evidence, when, and when the decision is reviewed again.

## Deprecation / Version Notes

Superseded versions, migration notes, and consumer notification.
