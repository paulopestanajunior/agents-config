# Humanize Writing Workflow

1. Identify the audience.
2. Identify the purpose of the text.
3. Identify the expected level of formality.
4. Preserve factual claims, terminology, constraints, and author intent.
5. Inspect the text for mechanical, generic, repetitive, or unnatural writing
   patterns.
6. Check for available voice samples or writing-style references.
7. Decide whether the edit should be light, natural, voice-matched, or deep.
8. Rewrite while preserving meaning.
9. Re-read the result as a human editor.
10. Remove remaining repetition, filler, generic phrasing, awkward rhythm, or
    unnecessary structure.
11. Verify that no facts, constraints, numbers, citations, code, commands,
    names, URLs, or important qualifications changed.
12. Return the final text.
13. For deep review, optionally explain the highest-impact editorial changes.

## Quality Check

- Does the text get to the point naturally?
- Does each paragraph add something new?
- Are sentence lengths varied where appropriate?
- Are transitions necessary and natural?
- Is the vocabulary specific rather than generic?
- Does the tone fit the audience?
- Does it sound like one coherent author?
- Were any facts changed?
- Was any important technical precision lost?
- Were any fake human traits introduced?
- If a writing sample exists, does the rewrite reasonably match it?
