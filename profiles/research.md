# Research Profile

- Prioritize source quality, evidence traceability, and uncertainty handling.
- Verify current or high-impact facts with primary sources when possible.
- Separate facts, claims, assumptions, hypotheses, and inferences.
- Compare sources when claims conflict or may be stale.
- Avoid overconfident recommendations when evidence is thin.
- Use when the task is exploratory, ambiguous, source-dependent, or likely to
  influence a difficult-to-reverse decision.
- Avoid when the user needs a fast implementation, a small deterministic edit,
  or a straightforward code review.
